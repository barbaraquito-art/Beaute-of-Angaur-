Getting Here

Transportation to Angaur

Visitors and residents can travel to Angaur by air or sea, with regular services from Koror.

By air

Pacific Mission Aviation (PMA)

Pacific Mission Aviation (PMA) provides scheduled air service between Koror and Angaur.

Scheduled flights

Tuesdays

  • Koror → Angaur9:00 AM
  • Angaur → Koror9:30 AM

Saturdays

  • Koror → Angaur9:00 AM
  • Angaur → Koror11:30 AM

Passenger fares (one-way)

  • Local residents$55
  • Non-residents / tourists (without permit)$110

Baggage allowance

  • First 30 lbs.Free
  • Excess baggage$0.70 per lb.

Charter service

  • Local charter$300 one-way
  • Non-local charter$400 one-way

Maximum capacity: 5 passengers and 1,000 lbs. total weight (including cargo).

Reservations & information

Pacific Mission Aviation
P.O. Box 906, Koror, Republic of Palau 96940
Website: pmapacific.org

Flight schedules and availability are subject to change. Advance reservations are recommended.

By sea

Angaur State Speedboat Service

The Angaur State Government operates regular speedboat services between Koror and Angaur for both passengers and cargo.

Current schedule

  • Thursday Angaur → Koror10:00 AM
  • Friday Koror → Angaur12:00 PM
  • Sunday Angaur → Koror12:00 Noon
  • Monday Koror → Angaur12:00 Noon

Service information

  • Passenger and cargo transportation available.
  • Advance reservations are required.
  • Cargo arrangements should be coordinated with the Transportation Office prior to departure.
  • Schedule may be adjusted due to weather or operational conditions.

Reservations & inquiries

Transportation Office
Malakal, Koror, Republic of Palau

Telephone(680) 488-5283(680) 488-5282

Please contact the Transportation Office to confirm schedules and make reservations before travel.
