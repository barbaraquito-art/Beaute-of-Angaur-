How your permit works

  1. Apply. Submit this form. You get your permit number straight away, with the status Pending payment, and a copy by email.
  2. Pay at the office. Visit the Medalii Office in Koror and see Teresa or Barbara. Bring your permit number.
  3. Permit card. Once payment is confirmed, your permit card appears at your private status link, ready to print.

Your permit is valid only after payment, when your permit card is issued. Your details are kept by the State office and used only to process your visit.

Questions? Contact the State office.
