Discover Angaur

Palau's Southernmost
Hidden Paradise

Nestled at the southern tip of Palau's archipelago, Angaur is a world apart — a quiet island of just eight square kilometres where limestone cliffs meet turquoise waters, and ancient traditions thrive undisturbed.

With a population of barely 114 souls, this is one of the last truly untouched destinations in the Pacific. Here, the air carries the salt of the ocean, the forests hide centuries of history, and every corner of the island tells a story.
